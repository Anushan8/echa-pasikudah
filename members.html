Membership

Join the association

Membership connects your establishment to a collective effort to promote Pasikudah and improve the standards visitors experience. Applications are reviewed by the committee.

  • A collective voice on destination and industry matters.
  • Listing in the association’s member directory.
  • Participation in association events, briefings and training.
  • Coordination on destination promotion and shared media content.
  • Practical connection with other hospitality businesses on the coast.
Request an application How we work
Simple process

How to apply

  1. Get in touch

    Contact the secretariat to request the membership application and current subscription details.

  2. Submit your details

    Provide business registration information, property details and the documentation requested by the committee.

  3. Committee review

    Applications are considered by the committee. Approved members are added to the association’s directory.

Application requirements, fees and eligibility criteria must be confirmed by ECHA Pasikudah and are shown here as placeholders.
