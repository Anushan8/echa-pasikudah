Governance

Leadership & committee

The committee is responsible for representing members, setting priorities and carrying out the association’s objectives.

The committee

Elected by members, accountable to members

ECHA Pasikudah is led by a committee drawn from its member establishments. Office bearers are elected by the membership, and committee members serve on a voluntary basis alongside their day-to-day work in the industry.

The committee meets regularly to review the association’s programmes, agree positions on destination matters and report back to members. Day-to-day coordination is handled through the association’s secretariat.

Working portfolios

Where the committee focuses

Committee members take responsibility for specific areas of work, reporting progress to the wider membership.

  1. Membership & standards

    Reviewing applications, maintaining the member directory and supporting service quality.

  2. Events & programming

    Planning association events, industry briefings and destination activities.

  3. Communications & promotion

    Managing the association’s public presence, destination content and media relations.

  4. Finance & administration

    Subscriptions, budgeting, record keeping and compliance with the association’s rules.
