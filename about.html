The Association

A shared voice for Pasikudah hospitality

ECHA Pasikudah brings hotels, guest houses and hospitality partners together to represent the destination, support its members and welcome visitors with confidence.

Who we are

An association built around one bay

ECHA Pasikudah is a hospitality association representing establishments on the Pasikudah coast in Sri Lanka’s Eastern Province. Its members include hotels, guest houses and hospitality businesses whose work depends on the destination’s reputation.

The association provides a forum where members coordinate on destination promotion, service standards and shared concerns. It also represents the Pasikudah hospitality community in dealings with tourism authorities, industry partners and the travelling public.

Because Pasikudah’s success is collective, the association works on the things no single property can achieve alone: consistent presentation of the destination, a reliable standard of welcome and a constructive relationship with the community that hosts visitors.

Our role

What the association does

Four areas of work sit at the centre of the association’s purpose. The detail of each programme is set by the committee and published to members.

  1. Representation

    Speak with one voice for Pasikudah’s hospitality establishments in discussions with authorities, industry bodies and partners.

  2. Destination promotion

    Coordinate a consistent picture of Pasikudah for domestic and international travellers, travel trade and media.

  3. Standards & training

    Encourage a dependable guest experience through shared expectations, staff development and peer learning.

  4. Collaboration

    Connect members with operators, suppliers, community groups and each other so that effort is not duplicated.

Our principles

Hospitality that respects the place

Pasikudah’s calm water and long beach are the foundation of its visitor economy. The association’s approach balances commercial hospitality with care for the bay, the community and the people who work in the industry.

  • Protect the quality of the bay and the beach that visitors come for.
  • Treat guests, staff and neighbours with the same courtesy.
  • Share information openly between member establishments.
  • Prefer long-term reputation over short-term occupancy.

“A destination is judged not by a single hotel, but by the experience a visitor has from the moment they arrive.”
