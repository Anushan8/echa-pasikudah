The Association

Welcome to ECHA Pasikudah

ECHA Pasikudah brings together hotels, guest houses and hospitality businesses along the Pasikudah coast, giving the destination a shared voice and a common standard of welcome.

The association works with its members and with tourism partners to promote Pasikudah as a distinctive east-coast destination — a calm bay, a long beach and a community that depends on hospitality done well.

Collective voice

Representing member establishments in destination and industry discussions.

Shared standards

The Destination

A quiet bay on Sri Lanka’s east coast

Pasikudah is known for a long, shallow bay where the water stays calm and clear well beyond the shoreline — a rare quality that has made it one of the east coast’s most established holiday destinations.

Province Eastern Province, Sri Lanka
Nearest town Batticaloa, approx. 35 km south
Character Calm, shallow, family-friendly water
Nearby Kalkudah Bay & lagoon country

Beyond the beach, the district offers lagoon landscapes, local markets, temple festivals and a slower rhythm of life that rewards travellers who stay a little longer.

Experiences & Activities

More than a stay

Pasikudah rewards travellers who look beyond the sun lounger. Here is how guests typically spend their days on the east coast.

Explore Activities

Beach Activities

Long morning walks, calm-water swimming and shaded afternoons on the sand.

Water Sports

Kayaking, sailing and gentle water-based activities suited to the sheltered bay.

Diving & Snorkelling

Warmer, clearer water in season, with reef and wreck diving arranged locally.

Local Culture

Temples, markets and festivals that reflect the district’s Tamil and Muslim heritage.

Nature & Wildlife

Lagoon birdlife, palm-lined backwaters and the quiet countryside inland.

Dining Experiences

Fresh seafood, Sri Lankan rice and curry and beachfront dining at member hotels.

Why Pasikudah

A destination with room to breathe

Pasikudah’s appeal is not a single attraction but a combination of geography, hospitality and pace — qualities the association works to protect.

01

A sheltered coastal bay

The reef-protected shoreline keeps the water calm and shallow, making the beach unusually easy to enjoy for families and weaker swimmers.

02

An established hospitality base

Pasikudah has a concentration of hotels and guest houses along the bay, supported by a local workforce with deep experience in tourism.

03

Room for every kind of traveller

From simple beachside guest houses to full-service resorts, the destination offers a range of accommodation rather than a single market.

04

Culture and nature close by

Kalkudah Bay, lagoon landscapes and the heritage of Batticaloa are within easy reach for half-day excursions.
