Vision, mission and objectives

The principles that guide ECHA Pasikudah’s work on behalf of the destination and its member establishments.

Our vision

What Pasikudah can become

To position Sri Lanka's East Coast as one of the most recognised, sought-after and sustainable tourism destinations in the region, celebrated for its natural beauty, rich cultural heritage, authentic experiences and warm Sri Lankan hospitality.

Our mission

How we work towards it

To unite hotels, tourism stakeholders and local communities through collective collaboration, strategic destination marketing and sustainable development, creating greater visibility, economic opportunities and memorable visitor experiences across Sri Lanka's East Coast.

Objectives

What we set out to achieve

ECHA working with six objectives guide the association’s activity. Specific targets, timelines and reporting will be published to members once approved.

  1. Destination Promotion

    Market the East Coast as a unified tourism destination, showcasing Pasikudah, Batticaloa, Trincomalee, Arugam Bay and surrounding attractions.

  2. Industry Collaboration

    Strengthen partnerships among member hotels, tourism operators, travel agents and other stakeholders.

  3. Digital Marketing

    Develop a strong online presence through the ECHA website, social media, shared image galleries and destination content.

  1. Sustainable Tourism

    Protect the coastline, marine ecosystems, natural resources and cultural heritage.

  2. Community Development

    Create employment, support local businesses and ensure tourism benefits surrounding communities.

  3. Industry Advocacy

    Represent members' interests and work with government authorities to improve tourism infrastructure, accessibility and regional development.
