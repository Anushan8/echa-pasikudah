The destination

Pasikudah, Sri Lanka

A shallow, reef-protected bay on the east coast, known for calm water, long beaches and a hospitality industry built around the sea.

The bay

Water that stays calm

Pasikudah sits on Sri Lanka’s east coast in the Batticaloa District, roughly 35 kilometres north of Batticaloa town. Its beach is sheltered by a reef, so the water remains shallow and unusually still well beyond the shoreline.

That geography has shaped the destination. Instead of a surf coast, Pasikudah offers a bay for swimming, paddling and long mornings in the water — qualities that suit families, couples and travellers looking for a quieter east-coast stay.

Behind the beach, coconut palms, small guest houses and a working local community sit alongside the hotels. Inland, lagoons and paddy country replace the coast, and the district’s Tamil and Muslim heritage is visible in its temples, mosques and markets.

Travel information

Getting to Pasikudah

Pasikudah is reached by road from Batticaloa. Most visitors arrive from Colombo or continue from the cultural triangle or the south coast.

By air

International arrivals land at Bandaranaike International Airport near Colombo. Domestic flights serve Batticaloa Airport, subject to current schedules.

From Colombo

The road journey is long — roughly six to seven hours depending on traffic and conditions. Many visitors break the trip or travel overnight.

From Batticaloa

Pasikudah is about 35 km north of Batticaloa town, reached via the A15. Allow around 45 to 60 minutes by road.

By rail, then road

Sri Lanka Railways operates services to Batticaloa. From the station, continue by road. Check current timetables before planning.

Distances, journey times and transport schedules are approximate and change with conditions. Confirm details with your accommodation or a local operator before travelling.
When to visit

Choosing your season

Sri Lanka’s two monsoons affect the coasts differently. The east coast’s main dry season falls in the middle of the year, when the southwest monsoon brings rain to the west and south.

May – September Main east-coast season: drier, sunnier
October – January Northeast monsoon: wetter, changeable seas
Year-round Warm temperatures; check local conditions

Sea conditions vary with the weather. Follow local advice and hotel guidance before swimming or taking part in water activities.
