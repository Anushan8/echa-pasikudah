SampleDestinationNovember 2026

Destination promotion: priorities for the season ahead

How the association intends to present Pasikudah to travellers, travel trade and media in the coming season — and why consistency matters for a destination of this size.

A destination is experienced through many small moments: the first message before arrival, the welcome at reception, the walk to the beach, the meal at the end of the day. Promotion that only shows the bay sets an expectation the whole destination has to meet.

The association’s approach is to coordinate the basics — a shared set of photographs, accurate seasonal information and a single directory of member establishments — so that partners and travellers receive the same picture of Pasikudah.
